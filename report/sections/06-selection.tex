\section{Machine Learning Model Selection}
\label{sec:selection}

\textbf{Criteria.} A scoring method for the Explorer had to (1) work without labels, (2) judge each transaction
against its own address's earlier history, (3) give a reason the user can check, (4) be stable, keeping a
top-50 overlap (Jaccard) of at least \V{sel.stability_target} across refits, (5) flag no more than
\V{sel.sanity} of any address's transactions, since an address flagged everywhere is simply different, and
(6) run on a laptop CPU. The brief adds that at least two of classification, clustering and regression are used.

\textbf{Candidates} (Table~\ref{tab:candidates}). No detector wins on every kind of anomaly (Han et al.\ 2022), so
each family covers a different idea of ``unusual''. The robust z baseline flags a transaction when any feature
lies far from the address's median. Clustering learns the shape of normal transactions and scores the distance
to it (Ikotun et al.\ 2023; Bushra and Yi 2021). Regression predicts each amount and gap from earlier history and
scores the miss; conformal calibration turns its quantile models into ranges with stated coverage (Angelopoulos
and Bates 2023). Isolation Forest and the Local Outlier Factor (LOF) are general outlier detectors (Boukerche,
Zheng and Alfandi 2021). TxGPT, a small transformer trained from scratch on DS2, reads a history as a sequence
and scores each transaction by its surprisal, the negative log-probability of what happened, as sequence models
do for event logs (Le and Zhang 2022). For labelled data we compared a linear model, a neighbour-based model and
two tree ensembles, which remain strong on tabular data (Grinsztajn, Oyallon and Varoquaux 2022).

\textbf{Tuning.} Every grid is in the settings file and every search in \code{tuning\_*.csv}, run on plant seeds
that never score reported results. After the first round, each method was retrained in stages, and a stage was
kept only if it passed a gate fixed in advance: higher recall with a bootstrap interval above zero, and no
pattern falling by more than its seed spread. LOF, K-Means and DBSCAN passed (\code{stages.csv}).

\textbf{The selection rule}, fixed in Assignment~1, picks the stable method with the highest mean planted recall,
and within five points the easier one to explain. Fusion scored highest (\V{ds1.fusion.mean}), but the baseline
(\V{ds1.baseline.mean}) lies within the margin, and the difference is not significant (paired bootstrap,
$p=\V{sig.fusion_p}$). The Explorer therefore uses the baseline, whose reasons map directly to the features.

\textbf{Explanations.} The \emph{Explorer Assistant}, a language model trained from scratch, may phrase each
explanation, but only if its answer passes a code guardrail: every number from the facts, the limitation line
present, no banned word, comparison words pointing the right way, and a verdict matching the flag. Otherwise a
fixed template is shown, because language models can state numbers absent from their input (Ji et al.\ 2023).
