\textbf{Communication rule:} The team holds one weekly decision meeting with a written agenda and genuine minutes (date, attendees, issues discussed, outcome, and each action with the member responsible and the time it was recorded), following the Week 2 communication guidance. Owners update the progress tracker before the meeting. Reviewers comment on evidence within one working day where possible; a blocked task is raised in the shared workspace the same day. Material scope or data changes go into the change log and require recorded agreement by the four members, with unresolved academic requirements referred to the tutor. Minutes are verified by attendees before they are attached.
