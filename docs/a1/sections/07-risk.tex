\section{Risk and quality management}
\label{sec:risk}

Table~\ref{tab:risks} lists the eight highest-priority risks; the full register of 22 risks is in Appendix~\ref{app:risk}.

Every risk is scored as \textbf{priority = likelihood × impact}, using one fixed mapping: likelihood Unlikely = 1, Low = 2, Moderate = 3, Likely = 4 and High = 4; impact Low = 2, Medium = 3, High = 4 and Critical = 5. The team reviews them weekly and after the data decision, consistent with the Week 3 risk and control material (Li n.d.e, slides 8–27). A mitigation reduces probability or impact; a fallback states what to do if the risk happens. Each risk keeps its register number, and its owner is named from the roles in Section~\ref{sec:team}.

\textbf{Quality plan:} Section authors verify claims against primary sources and add Harvard citations as they draft. A separate reviewer checks that requirements, WBS, dictionary and Gantt use the same scope and codes; the data lead checks feature definitions and leakage; the UX lead runs a scenario walkthrough; the leader conducts a final rubric/grammar/export review. In A2, a different member reproduces at least one sample and model result from the documented steps. Work is not marked complete merely because text or a graphic exists; the accepted evidence and its reviewer are linked in the progress tracker.
