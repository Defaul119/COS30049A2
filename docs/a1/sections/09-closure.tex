\section{Closure and acceptance}
\label{sec:closure}

Assignment 1 is approved for submission by the team when the requirements and rubric map are completed, WBS codes are consistent with dictionary and Gantt, the critical path calculation is verified, the major risks each have owner/trigger/response, the three prototype screens and feedback states are linked, and a teammate has reviewed and signed off the usability explanation. These are the planned checks derived from the Week 3 section of the discussion on acceptance and handover (Li n.d.e, slides 30–33). The leader verifies Harvard-style citations, the minimum 800-word requirement excluding references, the group-named filename, the signed contribution form and genuine weekly minutes, then checks the exported PDF for figure legibility and page breaks.

For the later integrated product: AC-01 to AC-03 above are achieved by providing documented sample records; the backend reproduces the indicator as displayed; failure and coverage states are shown; the model evaluation and known limitations are shown; and the handover is source, dataset manifest, setup steps and known limitations. A post-project review records what changed and what the team learned. The lecturer/tutor is the academic acceptance authority.
